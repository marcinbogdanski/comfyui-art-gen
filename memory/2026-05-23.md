# 2026-05-23

## CognitiveRiver Workflow Triage

- Investigated the local CognitiveRiver workflows and concluded the saved
  `cognitiveriver-simpler` and `cognitiveriver-adv` files are downstream
  refinement/control workflows, not complete provenance for the Civitai/reference
  images. The simple workflow behaves like a low-denoise img2img/upscale touch-up
  stage; the advanced workflow has switchable img2img/txt2img-style latent paths
  plus optional ControlNet/detailer/face tooling.
- Found that disabled/stale LoRA entries in the simple workflow should not be
  treated as required dependencies. The undercarriage concept appears in prompt
  text, not as an active LoRA dependency in the saved workflow.
- The A1111-imported `CLIPSetLastLayer` node broke CognitiveRiver txt2img output
  into gray/noisy images. Directly wiring checkpoint CLIP to `CLIPTextEncode`
  fixed generation; keep this in mind when adapting A1111-imported workflows for
  this checkpoint.
- Built and committed a cleaned CognitiveRiver ControlNet workflow export at
  `workflows/cognitiveriver/cognitiveriver_from_CyberRealistic_ctrlnet.my.json`
  in commit `cb62d3f`. It uses the CognitiveRiver checkpoint, HED-guided SDXL
  Union ProMax ControlNet, a first-stage batch, `ImageFromBatch`, Nickelback
  upscale, and a second low-denoise refine/save stage.
- Empirical ControlNet note: HED at strength `0.7` through `end_percent=1.0`
  copied too much guide-image background/clothing/edge detail. Softer settings
  around strength `0.35`, end `0.55` were adopted in the saved workflow. Even
  with ControlNet, coarse pose improves but small anatomy/detail failures remain
  expected for these complex prompts.
