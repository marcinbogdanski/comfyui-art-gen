# 2026-05-27

## Z-Image Turbo LoRA Experiments

- User experimented on May 26-27 with Z-Image Turbo plus ZIT LoRAs for
  explicit/creature scenes. Doggy-style composition was not automatic from pose
  words alone; it needed careful spatial wording such as describing hip contact.
- Mystic-XXX-ZIT-V7 was useful and should be kept available: it helped fix the
  recurring penis-stub artifact and worked at light strength. Z-Detail-Slider
  and zimage_lightslider were also useful. Civit_Art_V1T1 can be added lightly
  for a style touch.
- Position LoRAs tended to damage non-human/fantasy characters, especially
  zombies, by pulling them toward human forms. DOGGYSTYLE_ZIT and
  ZiTMythR3alisticF were deleted after testing; doggystyle_facing_camera remains
  downloaded but should be treated cautiously for fantasy/creature prompts.
- The full fp16 Moody Pro Mix ZIT V10 R1 DPO model was downloaded and verified,
  but user testing found dedicated porn models could destroy character identity,
  so do not assume it is a better default for these scenes.
- Demon/ogre/zombie dungeon scenes can work, but are fragile with LoRAs. Put
  creature identity and pose stability before heavy style/position LoRA stacks.
  Empty-room continuity worked when prompts included identifying room items.
- Simple one-person standing/sitting scenes were comparatively reliable. Avoid
  contradictory camera/composition instructions such as close-up plus full-body
  framing in the same prompt.
